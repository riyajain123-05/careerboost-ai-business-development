# CareerBoost AI

## AI-Powered Placement Preparation Platform

A Business Development, Selling & Negotiation project focused on building a full-funnel growth strategy for CareerBoost AI.

## Project Overview

CareerBoost AI is an AI-powered placement preparation platform designed for MBA students, fresh graduates, and internship seekers.

The platform addresses common placement challenges such as ATS-incompatible resumes, ineffective interview practice, low interview confidence, and lack of personalised feedback.

## Business Problem

Students often prepare for placements without knowing how recruiters evaluate them.

Key pain points identified:

- Poor resume structure and ATS compatibility
- Lack of structured interview practice
- Low confidence during interviews
- Lack of personalised, recruiter-level feedback

## Target Market

### Primary Segments

- MBA students preparing for final placements
- Students preparing for internships and pre-placement interviews

### Secondary Segments

- MBA students across Delhi NCR B-schools
- Final-year undergraduate students
- Fresh graduates seeking employment

## Value Proposition

CareerBoost AI positions itself as a **data-driven placement preparation partner**, rather than simply a resume-building tool.

### USP

> The AI platform that simulates real recruiter evaluation and provides data-driven feedback to improve interview success.

## Product Features

### AI Resume Analyzer

Evaluates resumes using ATS-style screening criteria.

**Benefit:** Helps improve the probability of clearing the initial shortlisting stage.

### Mock Interview Simulator

Provides realistic HR and technical interview practice.

**Benefit:** Builds confidence through repeated practice.

### Personalised Feedback Reports

Provides response-level insights into strengths and weaknesses.

**Benefit:** Enables faster and more targeted improvement.

### Performance Analytics Dashboard

Tracks progress across practice sessions using measurable scores.

**Benefit:** Makes improvement visible and measurable.

## Competitive Differentiation

CareerBoost AI differentiates itself from traditional preparation through:

- 24/7 on-demand access
- Structured and data-driven feedback
- ATS compatibility scoring
- Unlimited practice sessions
- Role and sector-specific preparation

## Business Development Strategy

The project focuses on generating demand through useful interactions rather than aggressive selling.

### Lead Generation Channels

- Campus workshops
- Placement preparation seminars
- Resume review booths
- Peer referral programmes
- LinkedIn outreach
- Placement forums
- Student communities

The strategy is based on building trust before asking for a purchase.

## Sales Approach

### Cold Outreach

The outreach conversation begins with the student's placement problem and then introduces CareerBoost AI as a solution.

The first call-to-action is a low-effort **5-minute demo**.

### Professional Sales Pitch

The pitch focuses on the gap between how students prepare and how recruiters actually evaluate resumes and interviews.

CareerBoost AI helps students identify weaknesses, practise realistic interviews, and improve using measurable feedback.

## Sales Funnel

The full customer journey is:

**Awareness → Interest → Trial → Purchase → Retention**

Example funnel:

- Awareness: 1,000 students
- Interest: 300
- Trial: 150
- Purchase: 60
- Retention: 30

The project highlights **Trial → Purchase** as an important conversion stage because this is where the product must earn customer trust.

## Promotion Strategy

### Digital Promotion

- LinkedIn educational content
- WhatsApp student groups
- Placement preparation communities
- Useful resume and interview content

### Campus Promotion

- Resume Review Booths
- Mock Interview Challenge
- Career Preparation Workshops
- AI vs Human Interview Challenge

These activities are designed to make the product tangible and generate word-of-mouth.

## Objection Handling

The project also considers common buyer concerns related to:

- AI reliability
- Pricing
- Effectiveness
- Need for human feedback

The approach is to respond to objections with evidence, demonstrations, and the practical value of measurable improvement.

## Retention Strategy

Retention is supported through:

- Premium upgrades
- Renewals
- In-app milestones
- Referral rewards
- Alumni mentor network
- NPS surveys

The objective is to convert users into repeat customers and advocates.

- Revenue per user
- Churn
- Renewal rate
- NPS
- Referral conversion

## Innovative Growth Ideas

### AI vs Human Challenge
A live campus activity comparing AI interview evaluation with human evaluation.

### Resume Review Booth
Students receive an instant ATS score and improvement report.

### Placement Cell Partnerships
CareerBoost AI can work with placement offices to create institutional programmes.

### Alumni Mentor Network
Placed students become mentors and referral advocates for the platform.

## Strategic Objective

The overall goal is to move students through:

**Awareness → Engagement → Trial → Purchase → Retention → Advocacy**

The project connects business development, selling, promotion and customer retention into one growth system.
